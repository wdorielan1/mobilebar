On The Rocks is a fully insured, RAMP-certified bartending company for birthdays, graduations, bridal and baby showers, weddings and get-togethers.

Because of state laws and regulations, you supply the alcohol and we supply everything else: the bar, your bartenders, ice, mixers, garnishes and decor.

How it works

Four steps to a booked bar

  1. Check your date

    Tell us your name, the type of event, the date and the package you'd like.

  2. Sign the contract

    We draft a contract for your event and send it over to sign.

  3. Lock in your date

    A 50% deposit secures your date.

  4. Shop your list

    We send a custom shopping list so you can buy alcohol to your taste. The remaining 50% is due 30 days before your event.

Cancellations

Cancel more than 90 days before your event and we return 50% of your deposit. Within 90 days of your event, the deposit isn't refundable.
